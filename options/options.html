Capture once, reuse everywhere

Capsule Flow turns AI conversations into portable context you can drop into any other assistant.

  1. Open a chat on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Grok, Copilot, Poe or Le Chat.
  2. Capture it. Click the Capsules button bottom-right, then Capture chat (or press Alt+Shift+C).
  3. Inject it into another tool's chat box with one click, or drag the card straight onto the input.

How it works

Capsule Flow is a context-transfer layer between AI tools.

On any other website you can still highlight text, right-click and choose Save selection as capsule.

What's in a capsule

When you capture a chat, Capsule Flow reads the messages on the page and writes a brief locally (no AI calls, nothing leaves your browser):

  • Goal — your first request in the conversation.
  • Constraints & preferences — sentences where you said must, should, avoid, only, don't…
  • Key points — bullet points and headings from the latest answers.
  • Where we left off — your most recent message.
  • The recent messages (or the full transcript), trimmed to your length limit.

Everything is editable before and after saving, and earlier versions are kept so you can roll back.
